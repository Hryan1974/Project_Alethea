## 🏛️ Executive Summary & Core Framework
Modern institutional risk management faces unprecedented structural shifts driven by high-frequency liquidity cascades and digital asset tokenization. Traditional static risk-weighted asset (RWA) frameworks under Basel paradigms fail to capture instantaneous cross-asset correlation breakdowns.

**Project Alethea** introduces a continuous-time neuro-symbolic governance layer that dynamically evaluates system safety invariants while maintaining auditability across decentralized and institutional ledgers.

### Key Theoretical Contributions:
1. **Neuro-Symbolic Impossibility Theorem:** Proves that purely probabilistic deep autoregressive networks cannot guarantee zero-violation safety bounds under out-of-distribution regime shifts.
2. **Kinetic Risk Entropy (KRE):** Formulates a real-time entropy operator over system state manifolds to bound tail-risk amplification.
3. **Symbolic Veto Kernel:** Implements deterministic invariant checks running in parallel with semantic neural estimators.
