Section 1.0

Executive Summary & Epistemological Foundations

"Deep neural networks and LLMs, operating purely on continuous probabilistic vector spaces, are mathematically incapable of guaranteeing strict safety bounds during catastrophic non-stationary market regime shifts."

Modern algorithmic governance in systemic financial infrastructure relies heavily on probabilistic machine learning frameworks. However, **Project Alethea** rigorously proves that deep neural architectures possess an intrinsic limitation: under non-stationary market conditions characterized by non-zero Kinetic Risk Entropy ($\mathcal{H}_K$), the probability of a catastrophic bound violation $P(\text{violation}) > 0$ for any finite neural capacity.

To resolve this epistemological barrier, Project Alethea introduces a **Hybrid Split-Plane Architecture**. By separating semantic extraction (probabilistic) from mathematical invariant verification and regulatory veto execution (deterministic), the system achieves absolute zero-violation safety guarantees across critical financial operations.

Probabilistic Limitation

Continuous vector spaces smooth over sharp topological discontinuities present in legal and regulatory constraint spaces.

Split-Plane Separation

Strict decoupling of soft intent understanding (Neural) from hard rule execution (Symbolic Kernel).

Zero-Violation Safety

Guaranteed hard bounds enforcement regardless of neural noise, hallucinations, or adversarial inputs.
